Privacy Policy for Ultimate Counter App

Last Updated: November 2023

This privacy policy explains how we handle your data in the Ultimate Counter application.

1. Data Collection

Our app does not collect any personal information. All data stays on your device.

Local Storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to save:

  • Counter values
  • App settings (theme, sound preferences)
  • Counter history
  • Your preferences

This data never leaves your device.

Important: All data is stored locally in your browser. We don't have access to your counter data.

2. What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information
  • No email addresses
  • No location data
  • No contact information
  • No usage analytics
  • No third-party tracking

3. Permissions

The app may request these permissions (optional):

  • Vibration: For haptic feedback (mobile devices only)
  • Sound: For click sound effects
  • Clipboard: To copy counter values (only when you click "Copy")

4. Your Rights

  • Clear all data anytime using browser settings
  • Export your counter data anytime
  • Use the app completely offline
  • Delete the app anytime

5. Children's Privacy

This app is safe for all ages. No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, changes will be posted here. The "Last Updated" date will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy:

  • Email: privacy@ultimatecounter.app
  • Or open an issue on our GitHub repository
